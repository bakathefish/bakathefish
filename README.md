### Rudra Puri

IB student in Amritsar, India. I build hardware, firmware and the data pipelines behind them, and I run my school's Hack Club. Most of my time right now goes to astronomy and machine learning.

**Research**
- [redress](https://github.com/bakathefish/redress): code, tables and model weights for my ISEF 2026 project. Seven published photometric selections for JWST Little Red Dots re-implemented on one catalogue and tested against 151 spectroscopic sources, plus a learned ranker compared with them at equal cost. Paper in preparation.
- [Flood](https://github.com/bakathefish/Flood) (Sailaab): open Sentinel-1 SAR flood mapping for Punjab, a 2015 to 2025 hazard atlas, and a live trilingual flood monitor. Still being shipped.

**Software**
- [ASTRO-OS](https://github.com/bakathefish/ASTRO-OS): an Arch-based Linux distribution for astronomy and research work, with its own signed package repository and kernels. Beta released September 2026.
- [sotto](https://github.com/bakathefish/sotto): an open-source desktop overlay assistant that sees your screen, hears your meetings and answers from your own files. Bring your own key.
- [airlock](https://github.com/bakathefish/airlock): a small low-gravity platformer made in Godot for Hack Club Jumpstart. [Play it on itch.io](https://bakathefish.itch.io/airlock).

**Hardware** (PCBs designed in EasyEDA, firmware in C++)
- [LANYARD](https://github.com/bakathefish/LANYARD): e-ink lanyard you update from your phone over WiFi
- [ASTRO-PET](https://github.com/bakathefish/ASTRO-PET): a desk pet that reads real sky conditions and tells you whether tonight is worth a shoot
- [EBOOST-DEVBOARD](https://github.com/bakathefish/EBOOST-DEVBOARD): ESP32-S3 devboard with the booster circuit a raw e-paper panel needs
- [MORSEY](https://github.com/bakathefish/MORSEY): bare-PCB keychain that decodes Morse taps onto an OLED
- [LED-PANEL](https://github.com/bakathefish/LED-PANEL): 13 by 18 addressable RGB LED panel, 234 pixels on one data chain
